# AuditGen — Customer Discovery Script

> 25-minute call script for agency owners (the primary ICP). Not a
> demo. Not a pitch. The goal is to **listen**, validate that we
> understand their motion, and earn the right to a paid trial.
>
> Treat this as a learning instrument. Every call goes into a shared
> log. After 10 calls you should be able to point at one or two
> specific things to change in the product or the positioning.

---

## When to use this script

- Cold-outreach replies from an agency owner who asked "what is this?"
- Founder calls during the first-100-logos motion.
- Lapsed trial users you want to bring back.

## When NOT to use it

- Investor calls (use `AUDITGEN_INVESTOR_DEMO_SCRIPT.md`).
- Existing paid customers you're trying to expand (different script;
  outcome-focused).
- Enterprise / inside-sales teams (different ICP, different script).

---

## Pre-call setup (3 min)

- [ ] Pull their agency website. Skim: services, vertical focus, team
      size, named clients if any.
- [ ] Pull one of their own clients' websites. Run a quick AuditGen
      audit on it (in your own workspace). Have it ready to share if
      the call gets concrete.
- [ ] Open this script and a blank notes doc.
- [ ] Audio + microphone tested.

---

## The 25 minutes

### 0:00 — Open warm and short (90s)

> "Hey [name], appreciate the time. I'll keep this to 25 minutes. I
> want to learn how you run your sales motion. I'll show you what
> we've built only if it'll actually help; if not, no demo, you keep
> your time. Sound fair?"

Goal: lower their defenses. Set the contract: this is discovery, not
a pitch.

---

### 1:30 — The motion today (8 min)

Five questions, in order. Let them talk. Don't interrupt.

1. **"Walk me through how a new lead becomes a closed deal in your
   agency. Start from where the lead comes from."**

   Listen for: source (referrals / outbound / paid / partner /
   inbound), volume per month, cycle length, ACV.

2. **"Who's actually doing the outbound work? You, a salesperson, a
   VA, a tool?"**

   Listen for: ownership, time spent, friction points, what gets
   neglected.

3. **"What's your close rate from a cold list right now?"**

   If they don't know: that's the data. Note "no measurement."

4. **"What's the most common reason a deal dies?"**

   Listen for: pricing, timing, wrong-decision-maker, the prospect
   ghosts. Note exact words.

5. **"If a magic system could fix one part of this motion for you,
   what would you fix?"**

   This is the gold question. Note their exact phrasing. This is
   your wedge for follow-up.

---

### 9:30 — The wedge probe (4 min)

Now you know what they care about. Connect it to AuditGen with two
specific cues.

> "What I heard you say is [paraphrase their pain — exact words].
> Here's the part of AuditGen that addresses that. Want to see it for
> 90 seconds?"

If yes, share the screen:
- For "we waste time on cold leads we shouldn't be calling" →
  `/brief` action queue (call today / follow up / stuck hot).
- For "our pitch sounds generic" → `/prep/<lead>` personalization
  score + the audit-anchored pitch.
- For "we don't know what's working" →
  `/admin/insights/scoring` baseline + lift tables.
- For "we send proposals and they go quiet" → the action queue's
  "stuck hot" + stale-proposal follow-up cues.

If no demo: skip the screen, go to the next section. Don't push.

---

### 13:30 — The pricing surprise (3 min)

> "We charge agencies $99, $299, or $799 a month depending on volume.
> The middle tier is what most 1–10 person agencies use. There's a 14-
> day free trial — 50 leads, 25 audits, no credit card. The reason I'm
> mentioning it now is I want to see if pricing is a real objection
> before we get to a demo where you might agree just to be polite."

Listen for: silence (yes, that's fine), "that's actually less than I
expected" (great, you've cleared the price objection), or hesitation
("that's a lot for a 3-person agency" → use the GTM playbook §9
objection #3).

---

### 16:30 — The trial close, soft (5 min)

If the wedge resonated AND pricing isn't a wall:

> "Here's what I'd suggest: I'll set up your workspace today, you
> import 25 leads you'd actually call this week, and we'll see what
> happens in the first 5 days. If after 5 days you're not getting more
> done with AuditGen than you were before, you cancel, no
> conversation. Want to do that?"

If yes: get them set up while the call is still open.
If no: ask **one** open question — "What would make this a yes?" —
note the answer, end the call.

---

### 21:30 — Capture the call (3 min)

After they're off the line, write into your discovery log immediately:

- ICP fit score (0–10)
- Pain magnitude (0–10)
- Authority (Y/N)
- Timing (Y/N — buying in the next 30 days?)
- Channel (how they found you)
- Wedge they cared about (one sentence, their words)
- Pricing reaction (one word)
- Trial conversion decision (paid / scheduled trial / passed)
- Any specific product feature they asked for that you don't have
- Any specific objection you didn't have a clean answer to

---

## Post-call within 24 hours

- [ ] Send a short note (3 sentences max). Reference one specific
      thing they said. Attach the audit you pre-ran on one of their
      clients. Do not attach a deck.
- [ ] If they're in trial: handhold day 0–5. See
      `AUDITGEN_AGENCY_ONBOARDING.md`.
- [ ] Update the discovery log with the outcome.

---

## After 10 calls

Look for patterns. Write a one-page memo titled
`DISCOVERY_PATTERNS_2026_05.md` (or whatever the month is) with:

- Top 3 pains that came up unprompted in > 5 of 10 calls.
- Top 3 features customers asked for that we don't have.
- Top 3 objections we don't answer cleanly yet.
- One specific change to make: in product, in positioning, or in
  the script itself.

The memo is the deliverable, not the calls. The calls are the
data-gathering pass.

---

## Things you do NOT say on a discovery call

- "We use AI to..."  (it doesn't matter; they want outcomes)
- "Our roadmap includes..." (don't sell roadmap; sell today's product)
- "What would you pay?" (you set the price; this question signals
  weakness)
- "Let me show you everything." (you've already lost; show one thing)
- "We integrate with..." (only if they ask)
