# AuditGen — GTM & Sales Playbook

> Operational manual for selling AuditGen, dogfooding it on real
> prospects, and turning every signal into a closed loop. Pair with
> `AUDITGEN_INVESTOR_POSITIONING.md` (the why) and `AUDITGEN_SAAS_ROADMAP.md`
> (the what). This doc is the how.

---

## 0. Two-line summary

AuditGen is a sales operating system for boutique digital agencies
selling presence packages to local businesses. We sell it the same way
our buyers should be using it: real audit + clear pain + clear next
step.

---

## 1. Ideal customer profile (operational definition)

A prospect is **in ICP** when ALL are true:

1. Sells to local businesses (US, anywhere). Vertical doesn't matter
   for ICP qualification.
2. Team size 1–10.
3. ACV $500–$5,000.
4. Has a pipeline of 100+ named accounts they "should reach out to" but
   haven't.
5. Currently uses one of: spreadsheets, Notion, HubSpot Starter, no CRM.

A prospect is **out of ICP** when ANY are true:

- Enterprise sales motion (named-account, multi-stakeholder, 3+ month cycles).
- Pure DTC e-commerce (no outbound to businesses).
- Needs 50k+ leads imported in week 1 (we don't optimise for that yet).
- Hostile to AI / requires "no AI in our stack."

When out of ICP, **disqualify in the first 5 minutes**. Don't waste
their time or yours.

---

## 2. Wedge: the audit

Every conversation starts with a real audit. Not a demo. Not a deck.
An audit of their *current* prospect's site (or their own site) that
walks them through what AuditGen does in 90 seconds.

**Format we send:**

```
Hey [name],

I ran a quick AuditGen audit on [their lead | their site] and pulled
out the three things that are probably costing them money right now:

  1. [audit-specific issue]
  2. [audit-specific issue]
  3. [audit-specific issue]

The full audit + my recommended pitch is here: [signed link].

This is what AuditGen does for every lead in your list. If it lands,
want to see what it'd cost to run it on your top 500 leads?
```

**Anti-patterns** (do not send):
- "Hope this finds you well."
- Generic "I help agencies grow."
- A loom video that runs > 90 seconds before showing the audit.
- A pitch deck PDF as the first asset.

---

## 3. Conversation framework

The seven moments that matter, in order. Use this on every call.

| Moment | What to do | What "good" looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Open | Acknowledge their time. State the one thing you want from the call. | "I want to show you the audit and find out if 5 minutes of this would have changed your last 10 deals." |
| Diagnose | Ask: "what does your current motion look like for a new lead list?" | They describe a manual process. Don't pitch yet. |
| Show | Run an audit live on a lead THEY name. Use their lead, not yours. | 90 seconds end to end. They can see the gap. |
| Quantify | Ask: "how many of these are in your spreadsheet right now?" | They give a real number. Multiply by close-rate to anchor. |
| Frame | "This is what AuditGen does. The pitch you just saw is auto-generated for every lead." | Show the prep page. Not the dashboard. |
| Price | "Growth is $299/mo. Most agencies your size run it on 2,000 leads/mo." | One sentence. Don't justify. |
| Close | "Want me to set it up for you this afternoon? You'll have your first 50 audits by tomorrow." | Specific next step with a deadline. |

If they ask for a deck: send the investor positioning doc. If they ask
for a free trial: send the trial link (14 days, 50 leads, 25 audits).

---

## 4. Pricing posture

Anchored, never negotiated below the floor:

- **Starter $99** — anyone with a credit card. No discount.
- **Growth $299** — small agency. No discount. **This is the default
  recommendation.** If they push back on price, downgrade them to
  Starter; don't discount Growth.
- **Agency $799** — > 3-person agency, white-label needs. No discount
  in the first 6 months; annual discount is 2 months free.
- **Enterprise** — custom. Only quote after a real qualification call.

**Discount discipline:** If you cannot articulate why this prospect is
strategically worth a discount in one sentence, the discount is wrong.

## 5. Channel mix (first 90 days)

In order of expected return on the founder-led hour:

1. **Twitter / X**. Post real audits with permission. Use the public
   audit link. Pin one viral example. Aim for 3 posts/week. Inbound
   conversion: 1% of viewers to trial.
2. **LinkedIn DMs to agency owners.** Personalised, three sentences,
   one audit link. 30/day, 2% reply rate, 25% trial conversion of
   replies. ~5 trials/week at steady state.
3. **Communities** (agency Slack/Discord groups, indie hacker, vertical
   subreddits). Helpful answer + one audit example per week. Slow,
   compounding.
4. **Partner referrals.** One or two vertical-specialist agency
   communities embed AuditGen. Revenue share or flat per-logo bounty.
5. **Cold email at scale.** Last resort. Only after Phase 3 outreach
   personalisation ships so we're not sending the templated copy we
   tell prospects to stop sending.

**Don't do (yet):**
- Paid ads. Wait until the inbound funnel is measurable.
- SEO content marketing. Wait until the product is more stable; SEO is
  6–12 month investment.
- Conferences / events. Only the right ones; never the local-business
  expo circuit.

## 6. First-call qualification rubric

Score each prospect 0–10 on these five during the call:

| Signal | 0 | 5 | 10 |
|---|---|---|---|
| ICP fit | Out of ICP | Maybe ICP | Clearly ICP |
| Pain magnitude | "We're fine, just looking" | "Outreach is messy" | "We're losing deals because of [specific gap]" |
| Authority | Not a decision-maker | Influences | Owns budget |
| Timing | "Maybe in Q4" | "Soon" | "This week" |
| Channel fit | Found us by accident | Engaged | Referred by another customer |

**>= 30 total:** close them on the call. Send Stripe link.
**20–29:** schedule a follow-up audit on THEIR top lead, 5 days out.
**< 20:** disqualify. Send the trial link. Don't follow up unless they
write back.

---

## 7. Trial-to-paid conversion

The trial is 14 days, 50 leads, 25 audits. Conversion levers, in order:

1. **Day 0:** they import their first 50 leads. If they don't, the trial
   is dead. Hand-onboard if needed.
2. **Day 2:** they run their first 10 audits and see the prep pages.
   Email/DM with one specific call-out from their first lead's audit.
3. **Day 5:** they send their first outreach using an AuditGen prep.
   This is the moment they decide. If they did, conversion >50%. If
   they didn't, send them their best lead's audit + a "want me to walk
   through it?" reply.
4. **Day 10:** they get the first logged reply from a prospect. If
   they reply-log it inside AuditGen, conversion >70%. If not, send a
   manual "what did they say back?" check-in.
5. **Day 12:** "want to keep going? $299/mo, here's the link."

We measure: trial-to-paid by week, first-audit time, first-outreach time,
first-reply-log time. These are the only four numbers that matter in
the first 6 months.

---

## 8. Retention + expansion

Churn drivers we should monitor weekly:
- < 5 audits/week generated → at risk. Reach out.
- 0 replies logged in 14 days → at risk. Reach out.
- All-time close rate < 5% → product-fit problem, not them.
- Owner stops logging in → blocker, reach out.

Expansion plays, in order of payback:
1. **Seat add.** $299 → $799 when they hire #4.
2. **Vertical pack** (Phase 7). $799 + $99/vertical/mo.
3. **Enrichment credits.** $0.05/lead overage above plan.
4. **White-label audit pages** (Agency tier).
5. **CRM integration** (HubSpot / Pipedrive). $99/mo connector.

## 9. Objection handling (top 10)

1. **"This is just an AI wrapper."**
   → "The wrapper is the audit. What's underneath is the loop. You'll
   stop noticing the AI by day 5 because you'll be looking at your
   pipeline numbers, not the prompts."

2. **"We already have HubSpot."**
   → "HubSpot is where deals live. AuditGen is what gets them there.
   You'll keep HubSpot."

3. **"$299 is a lot for a 3-person agency."**
   → "If it closes one extra $1,500 deal a month, it pays for itself
   5x. If it doesn't, I'll cancel you. What's the math on your last 10
   deals?"

4. **"The audit will sound robotic / our clients will know it's AI."**
   → "Run it on your top lead and tell me which sentence reads
   robotic. If you can point to one, I'll rewrite it. If you can't,
   we have a deal."

5. **"We tried another AI sales tool. Didn't stick."**
   → "Which one? Apollo / Clay / etc. don't run the loop. They generate
   copy. AuditGen ties copy to pipeline to outcomes."

6. **"Send me a deck."**
   → Send the investor positioning doc + one real audit. "Read the
   audit first."

7. **"Can you do a custom thing for us?"**
   → "Not yet. If your version of the workflow becomes a vertical pack
   in 90 days, you get free use of the pack for a year."

8. **"What about security / compliance?"**
   → "Workspace-isolated. Signed audit links. HMAC webhook ingestion.
   Scrubbed session tokens. Admin allowlist. Happy to send the
   security posture doc."

9. **"We need it to integrate with [CRM]."**
   → "We have public ingest + outbound webhooks. For HubSpot /
   Pipedrive native connectors, ETA Q2."

10. **"What if I just want the audits, not the rest?"**
    → "Starter at $99. Up to you whether you use the loop. Most
    people who start with just audits move to Growth in 60 days."

## 10. Daily / weekly cadence

**Daily (founder, 60 min):**
- 30 min: respond to inbound (Twitter DMs, email replies).
- 20 min: post one audit example or one lesson on Twitter.
- 10 min: log replies on every trial customer who responded.

**Weekly (Monday, 90 min):**
- Run the funnel: how many new logos, how many at risk, what's the
  reply rate this week?
- One product decision based on the week's data. Write it down.
- Update `DAILY_OPENCLAW_REPORT.md` with the week's wins + losses.

**Monthly (first of month, half day):**
- Review the close-rate-by-vertical / city dashboard
  (`feature/outcome-analytics-by-vertical-city`).
- Decide which vertical to invest in next.
- Update pricing tiers if data justifies. Don't change pricing more
  than once per quarter.

---

## 11. What we don't do

To stay sharp:

- No undefined "we'll figure it out" pricing for whales. Either
  Enterprise quote or no deal.
- No cold outreach with templated copy. Eat our own dog food.
- No discounting Growth.
- No free pilots that don't have a date for "→ paid" attached.
- No multi-month rollout plans for SMB customers. They use it in week
  one or they churn.
- No marketing copy that exaggerates close-rate uplift. The product
  speaks; the loop measures.

---

## 12. Metrics we live and die by

In order of priority:

1. **New paying logos / week.** Goal: 5/week by day 60, 15/week by day 90.
2. **Trial-to-paid conversion.** Goal: 30%+.
3. **Reply-log rate per active workspace.** Goal: 50% of contacted
   leads have a logged reply.
4. **Audits generated / workspace / week.** Goal: 30+ by week 2 of
   trial; 80+ for paid Growth.
5. **Monthly logo churn.** Goal: < 5%.
6. **NRR (net revenue retention).** Goal: > 110% by month 6.

These six numbers go in a single dashboard. If a feature doesn't move
one of them, it doesn't ship until the loop is closed.
